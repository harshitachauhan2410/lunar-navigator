About the model

This tool uses an analytic approximation of the Sun’s and Earth’s position in the lunar sky (mean orbital elements, libration to first order). It is accurate to roughly a degree or so, good for exploring and comparing options, not for flight operations. The terrain slider applies one uniform horizon angle. Roadmap: real LOLA topography per site, SPICE ephemerides, shadow maps for polar craters, and sharing a site by link.
